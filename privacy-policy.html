Overview

🔒 Your data stays yours

This policy explains what SHARDMARCH: Match 3 Heroes handles, what it does not, and what choices you have. It is written to be read.

The short version: the developer collects nothing about you. The game has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics, and your progress never leaves your device. The one exception is advertising: the game offers optional rewarded ads supplied by Google AdMob, and Google collects data in order to deliver them.

Data Storage

Your campaign stars, unlocked stages, castle upgrades and settings are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded, and the developer cannot see them.

Your answer to the ad-consent question is also stored on the device, so you are not asked again on every launch. Uninstalling the game removes all of it.

What the Developer Collects

Nothing. SHARDMARCH does not ask for, receive or store:

  • Your name, email address or any contact information
  • Any account or login of any kind
  • Location data
  • Gameplay telemetry, analytics or crash reports
  • Payment information — the game has no in-app purchases

The developer sees only aggregate, anonymous advertising earnings figures provided by Google. These cannot identify any individual player.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads. You are never required to watch one, and no part of the game is locked behind them. The full campaign is playable without ever seeing an ad.

When an ad is requested, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • Device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • Your IP address, and the approximate region derived from it
  • Whether the ad was viewed or clicked

Google handles this data as an independent controller. Its use is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and by how Google uses data from apps that use its services. The developer does not receive it.

Your Consent Choice

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's official consent form before any ad is requested, and asks your permission first.

This version of the game does not yet include an in-game control for changing that answer afterwards. Until it does, you can:

  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings, under Google → Ads
  • Clear the game's app data, which clears the stored answer so you are asked again on next launch

An in-game privacy option is planned for a future update, and this page will be updated when it ships.

Permissions

The game requests exactly one Android permission: internet access, used only to load ads. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or file access.

Children's Privacy

SHARDMARCH is rated for teens and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the address below and it will be addressed.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes will also be noted in the game's release notes on Google Play.
